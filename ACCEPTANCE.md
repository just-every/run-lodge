# Lodge acceptance record

This record matches every named clause in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md). Status uses **done**, **partial** and **missing** at the judged code commit in the contract. Done means existing local code and a public-door test cover the stated local guarantee; partial names useful implementation but leaves a material part of the journey unbuilt; missing has no implementation of the promised outcome. These are implementation judgments, not claims that target provider or legal acceptance has happened.

The preserved LDG flows use the real Worker and D1 through native sessions, the published rer verbs and built browser pages. Tests fake only the invitation/mail network edge where required. The Workable importer is tested against documented-shape fixtures; a real authorized import/export-back comparison remains MIG-007 acceptance. New journeys below describe required evidence for a build round, not tests that already exist.

> **COM-005 — Automatic application acknowledgments and status emails.**
>
> Employers can enable an acknowledgment email per job, from an employer-reviewed template in the applicant's recorded ATTR-007 language, sent once when an application is received, and optional approved status emails on chosen stage changes, with recorded outcomes. The LDG-003 receipt remains the authoritative confirmation and includes its receipt code; disabling or failing email never removes the receipt page or prevents a valid application. English and Spanish and other supported languages retain the complete ATTR-007 review and version requirements.
>
> Supported installation-mail default: application acknowledgments and approved factual stage-status emails use Base SendGrid. This transport is an increment within COM-005 and does not replace or complete the COM-001 target of connected Gmail and Microsoft 365 mail, replies, attachments, threading, mailbox-side synchronization and reconciliation. COM-002 through COM-004 and every other communications target remain unchanged. Installation-mail delivery alone does not prove those targets.
>
> Sending starts off for the installation and every job. Only a current human owner or hiring admin with access to the job can enable sending or approve its automation configuration; managers, interviewers and agents cannot enable it. Approval binds the exact template version, application language, subject, body, permitted merge fields, sender, reply-to, job, trigger and timing. Permitted merge values are candidate name, job title and business name, plus the receipt code for acknowledgments and an employer-reviewed public status label for stage mail; restricted answers, private notes, scores and decision reasons are unavailable. The preview shows filled recipient and text, required footer and missing values. Missing values, unknown fields, missing or stale language review, changed approved configuration or lost authority block sending visibly. Job-specific overrides require the same review; global edits never silently change a job's approved version or queued text.
>
> Automatically send only an enabled application acknowledgment or an explicitly approved factual status template on a committed stage event. Default acknowledgment timing is immediate after accepted intake; optional stage automation is off until configured. Stage mail may use immediate or explicitly reviewed delayed timing in the business time zone, with daylight-saving behavior, eligible weekdays and cancellation shown in preview. DISP-001 rejection timing remains its existing 1 to 20 business days and its existing email choice. Template changes do not alter already approved queued text. Recheck current stage, authority, contact preference, configuration and candidate existence before dispatch; cancel obsolete stage mail after another stage change, withdrawal or hire. Imported history, retries and configuration edits do not trigger retrospective mail.
>
> Automatic mail never decides progression, rejection or suitability and never invents a reason. Personalized or agent-generated text, unreviewed language, offers and substantive hiring decisions remain drafts for the applicable human approval. An already authorized rejection remains governed by DISP-001 and SCREEN-002, including approved automatic rejection rules; COM-005 creates no new rejection authority. Existing LDG-010 exact-message approval and unsent-download guarantees remain intact. AUTO-001 actions and COORD-005 silence monitoring retain their own full targets and approval rules.
>
> Use an employer-approved authenticated installation sender and a monitored employer reply-to. Block production sending until sender identity and domain authentication required by Base SendGrid are verified; show the configured sender and reply-to before enablement. Do not impersonate a recruiter's connected mailbox or claim authenticated employer-domain sending without proof. Replies must reach the monitored reply-to; automatic ingestion, two-way conversation and mailbox readback remain COM-001 Gaps until proven. An unsupported reply route blocks enablement.
>
> Honor candidate opt-out and employer contact restrictions and provider suppression for complaints, hard bounces and invalid addresses before enqueue and again before dispatch. Do not bypass a suppression through another template, job or transport. Erasure cancels and removes candidate-linked pending mail and personal message data under LDG-012; retain only an approved minimal non-reversible suppression mechanism and personal-data-free audit metadata consistent with that clause. A restored archive or replay must not resurrect erased recipients. Do not claim suppression for earlier erasures whose address cannot be recovered safely. Employer-reviewed content must include applicable identification, contact details, physical address, opt-out mechanism and other legally required content for the message's purpose and jurisdiction. The employer owns legal classification, required content, retention and review; Lodge does not certify CAN-SPAM, state-law or employment-law compliance.
>
> Persist durable duplicate protection for each application acknowledgment and each approved stage-event purpose across concurrent intake, webhook replay, scheduler restart and alternative send paths. Retrying or editing a template does not create another acknowledgment for the same application. Record frozen message version, language, authorized configuration, event, due time and attempts under existing access and erasure rules. Pending, suppressed, canceled, provider-accepted, delivered when verified, bounced, failed and uncertain are distinct visible outcomes. Provider acceptance is not recipient delivery. An uncertain send is held for reconciliation and never blindly retried. Failures and bounces identify an authorized human responsible for resolution without exposing confidential candidate data.
>
> After restore, installation and job automatic sending remain off, archived pending mail is inert and no backlog sends automatically. A current authorized human admin must review current sender, language, suppression and queued-event state before explicitly enabling future sending. Preserve duplicate protection and erasure guarantees. COM-005 configuration and production attempts inherit OPS-003 isolated trial, exact-reviewed activation, rollback, employer confirmation of required provider plan and access, reviewed legal setup and transparent USD estimates, and OPS-001 audit. This does not introduce a new owner-confirmation gate or authorize a provider request.
>
> Required journey: Enable an acknowledgment template and submit an application: one email arrives with the receipt code; replay the same submission and receive no second email; disable it and confirm a new applicant gets the receipt page only. Also prove per-job override isolation, approved chosen-stage mail and cancellation, current human admin authority, stale-review refusal, missing-field refusal, English and Spanish reviewed language, authenticated sender and monitored reply-to, opt-out and bounce suppression, erased-candidate refusal, concurrent and restarted duplicate protection, uncertain-send reconciliation, honest failure and delivery outcomes, inert imported history and sending off after restore. Trial and roll back the exact configuration without contacting production recipients. Capture every changed screen at widths 1440 and 390. Preserve the complete original acceptance journey and all inherited obligations; every unproved item remains a Gap.

> **REPORT-003 — Scheduled private recruiting reports.**
>
> **CT ruling, 26 October 2026: ADOPTED WITH RIDERS; implementation status remains partial.** The initial native weekly email default is configured and explicitly enabled only by a current human owner or hiring admin with access to every selected job. Review records the configuring human and the exact selected jobs, metrics, recipients, sender, cadence, business time zone and recipient-specific preview. Changed configuration requires fresh review; activation and dispatch recheck current authority and relevant configuration revisions.
>
> Recipients are active native human recruiting members with current report access and export authority on every selected job. Hiring managers qualify when those conditions hold. Interviewer status, limited activity-export rights, employee membership, an agent identity, an external address or a historic grant do not by themselves qualify. Assignment-scoped interviewer reporting remains a named Gap. External agencies remain within CRM-005: this default grants no employer-report access; a future agency-specific digest must preserve its existing invited-job, frozen-submission and permitted-progress boundaries.
>
> At dispatch, compute each recipient's report independently from the intersection of the current configuring author's and recipient's authorized data, including custom permission levels, confidential jobs, private candidates and feedback-release rules. Never combine their grants or reuse another recipient's result. If either person loses required authority for a selected job, visibly withhold that recipient's delivery until the configuration is reviewed; do not silently widen access or change the approved selection.
>
> Email contains aggregates only: no candidate identifiers, contact details, individual records, decision reasons, restricted answers, private notes or unreleased feedback. Aggregates are not automatically anonymous. Apply suppression or coarsening where small cells, narrow cohorts, report differences or revealing labels could disclose protected or private information. Keep useful source and funnel measures, definitions, denominators, period, time-zone and cohort filters, freshness and visible suppression or scope limits. Links require native authentication and current authorization at opening, drill-down and download; possession of a link never grants access.
>
> Recipient, author or connector authority loss suspends affected sending. An uncertain provider outcome is held for reconciliation and never automatically replayed. Restored authority alone does not resume sending. A currently authorized human administrator reviews the current configuration, sender, recipients, scope and outstanding claims before explicitly enabling future periods. Reconcile an uncertain prior period before any further attempt for that period; unresolved uncertainty remains held. Preserve durable duplicate protection across concurrent claims, restart, reconciliation and restore. Restored sending remains inactive until reviewed.
>
> REPORT-003 inherits existing access, independent-feedback, candidate-data, audit, retention, holds, erasure, portability, sandbox, provider-setup and human employment-decision obligations from their respective clauses. Recompute or invalidate retained reports as those obligations require. Local erasure cannot retract already delivered external copies; the employer retains responsibility for their custody. This ruling grants no compliance certification and does not complete or replace COM-001, COM-004 or COM-005.
>
> The complete target remains recipient-scoped dashboards on an employer-selected schedule by email or Slack, with time-zone-aware cadence, metric definitions, period, authenticated drill links, permission checks at send and download, private-data redaction, visible delivery outcomes and suspension after revocation. Required acceptance remains: schedule a weekly source and funnel report to email and Slack, revoke one recipient before the due time and restart; eligible people receive the correct period once and the revoked person cannot receive or open restricted data. The weekly native email default does not replace general dashboard selections, other cadences, Slack or any original acceptance point.

## Running the evidence

`pnpm test:lodge` runs the eight Lodge HTTP/D1 suites with one test worker. `tests/browser.mjs` exercises the committed checkout at 1440 and 390 pixels, including manager, approver, applicant and interviewer journeys and archive round-trip. `pnpm exec rer check --profile first-party` checks the contract/status shape and evidence paths. `rer verify` runs builds, typecheck, flow suites, emitted-runtime checks and Base hosted-output/hydration checks in CI; the browser journey also runs on every push. Only run relevant checks locally; CI is the full wall.

Provider acceptance uses authorized real accounts and candidate-controlled test recipients: read back board listings, mail threads, calendar events, video join details, DocuSign artifacts and HRIS identity/terms. Network-edge fault cases supplement that proof. A timeout, downloaded file, generated task or acknowledged request cannot prove delivery, signature, employee creation or completed coordination. New app, mobile, legal and migration acceptance stays pending until its named journey is evidenced.

## Boundaries

Local proof does not establish hosted deployment, provider delivery, universal US legal compliance or acceptance of a real incumbent migration. Native apps require installed-device evidence; EEO/OFCCP and recording/background-check configurations require employer legal review. Human hiring decisions, independent feedback and access/erasure guarantees apply throughout target execution. No UI, workflow, test or homepage behavior is changed by this record.
