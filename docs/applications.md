# Applications

Take applications from your page or add candidates yourself, then move each
one through your stages with a reason.

## The application page

`/jobs` lists your published vacancies. Each page works without JavaScript,
labels every field, shows your accommodation contact and the current privacy
notice, and gives the applicant a receipt. Answers about adjustments and
voluntary monitoring are kept as restricted records that only the owner and
hiring admins can see.

## Add a candidate yourself

Under **Candidates**, use **Add candidate** for a referral or someone you met.
Record their **Source (where they came from)**, the **Basis for keeping their
details** and a **Note about consent**, link them to a vacancy with **Apply to
vacancy**, and attach their files. Downloads always return the original file.

One person can hold applications for several vacancies. When two records look
like the same person (same email, phone or name), Lodge lists them under
**Possible duplicates** with the reason. Nothing is merged until a person
decides.

## Move an application

Open **Applications** and press **Open** on a row. The application shows its
**History**: every stage move with who made it, when and why.

To move it, choose **Move to** and give a **Reason (kept in the history)**.
Closing it as *Not progressing* or *Candidate withdrew* also needs a reason.
If two people move the same application at once, only one move is kept and the
history stays one unbroken chain.

## Assign reviewers

**Reviewers (emails, comma separated)** chooses who may read the application.
Interviewers see only the applications they are assigned to, without contact
details or restricted documents.

## Candidate account updates

An invited candidate signs in at `/candidate`, opens their receipt and chooses
**Your application**. An owner or hiring admin uses **Publish candidate progress
and request information** on the application to share a coarse update and ask
for ordinary information. Nothing publishes the internal stage or employer notes.
The candidate answers each request once; the original submission stays intact.

Candidates save their preferred contact method and optional consent for other
roles. Changing an email needs a sign-in within the last 15 minutes and a
confirmation sent to the new address. Confirm while still signed in to that
candidate account. Other sessions and old sign-in links end; existing accounts
are never merged. The verification email follows the application language;
English and Spanish system copy is included. Other languages require a reviewed
`candidate-email-copy` version through the documented command API.

**Withdraw application** needs an explicit confirmation. It closes this application,
cancels pending contact, tasks and interviews, and preserves submitted evidence,
legal holds and retention.
