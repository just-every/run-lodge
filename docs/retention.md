# Retention and deletion

Keep candidate records only as long as you need them, and keep a receipt when
you delete.

## Past their retention date

**Retention** lists candidates past the date in **Keep candidate records for
(days)**. To delete one, type their name exactly, give a reason and press
**Delete**. Deletion needs every application closed. It removes their records
and files and leaves a receipt with counts, but no personal details.

## Legal holds

**Place hold** on a candidate or a vacancy, with a reason, to stop deletion.
**Release hold** needs a reason too.

## Candidate requests

Record an access, export, correction or deletion request under **Candidate
requests**. For access and export, **Download their data and complete** gives
the candidate's records and original files in one download and completes the
request.

Retention and recording rules differ by place: have them reviewed for where
you hire before real candidates apply.
