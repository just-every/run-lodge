# Interviews and scorecards

Plan interviews from the vacancy's plan, agree a time, and let each interviewer
score on their own.

## Plan an interview

On an open application, choose **Plan an interview**. Pick the **Interview**
from the vacancy's plan, list the **Interviewers (emails, comma separated)**,
offer up to three proposed times and a location or video link, then press
**Propose interview**.

Each interviewer ticks the **Times I can attend** and presses **Save my
availability**. The candidate answers through a private availability link,
which you include in an approved message. When a time suits everyone, choose
the **Confirmed time** and press **Schedule**.

**Download calendar invitation (.ics)** gives a standard calendar file to add to
any calendar. Lodge sends no invitations itself.

## Let the candidate book

In the application’s **Interviews** tab, tick the option to share the interview’s
title, location and panel-approved times, then press **Save candidate booking**.
Every panel member must record availability first. The candidate signs in at
`/candidate`, opens **Your application**, and chooses the interview. They can
**Book interview** or **Reschedule interview** using the offered future times.
Lodge refuses stale pages, closed applications and overlapping local panel or
candidate appointments. Changing the time updates the same interview and keeps
its history. Turning booking off stops further candidate bookings.

These are times recorded in Lodge. Connected-calendar availability and delivered
calendar invitations are separate capabilities.

## Write a scorecard

Each interviewer rates every criterion of the application's rubric from 1 to 4
(or leaves it blank if they did not assess it), writes the evidence for each
rating, gives a **Recommendation** and presses **Save scorecard**. Ticking
**Submit (you cannot change it afterwards)** makes it final.

**Your private interview notes** are visible only to you.

## Release feedback

Each scorecard stays private to its author until the manager releases
feedback. Until then nobody else, the manager included, can read it. Press
**Release feedback to the panel** once everyone has submitted; releasing earlier
needs a reason.

The **Overview** shows who has not yet submitted under **Coordination**.

## Debrief brief

After release, your coding agent (or you) can prepare a debrief brief through
the `debrief` command (the repository's `API.md` describes it). It lists, per
criterion, where the interviewers agree or disagree, criteria nobody assessed and questions to
resolve, each statement quoting the interviewers' own released words. Lodge
refuses a statement it cannot match to a quote, a missed disagreement or gap,
and any combined score, recommendation or protected-trait reference. It shows
under **Scorecards** for the people who run the job; edit it, then keep or
discard it. It never moves the candidate.
