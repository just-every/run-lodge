#### ATTR-015 — Current office authority for reviewed native job listings

*Tier:* Core · *From:* new

**Requirement.** Employers can review native structured job listings using the reviewer's current effective settings authority for the job's office, including office-level grants. Publication remains bound to that current authority and complete, accurate job metadata.

**Acceptance.**
1. An active manager with current office-granted settings authority can review an eligible selected approved live post in that office. Its public careers page emits exactly one accurate, current, employer-reviewed native JobPosting with complete required metadata, just as for the authorized owner; ordinary job fields and structured values agree.
2. Publication uses the reviewer's current effective authority, including office-level grants. Revocation, deactivation or loss of required settings authority suppresses the formerly reviewed structured listing while the otherwise public job page remains readable, without leaking authority information.
3. A person without current authority cannot create or refresh the review; refusal leaves review state and public markup unchanged. Current authorized owners and office-granted managers can still review, with ordinary job fields and structured values consistent.
4. The supported native review form and public listing accurately show the resulting state at 1440 and 390 pixels. Local structured markup makes no claim of live Google admission.
