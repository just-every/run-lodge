# Lodge target product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Lodge is a complete SMB applicant tracking and recruiting product: attract and source applicants, communicate continuously, coordinate interviews, collect independent evidence, obtain signed offers and verify handoff into HR. A US internal recruiter or operations lead at a 20–200 employee employer can switch from the named ATS and recruiting tools below. USD is the default for pay, budgets and costs; US time zones, daylight saving and employer-reviewed state/location requirements are explicit.

This contract describes the product being built towards. The status table separates current local implementation from missing execution; difficulty obtaining provider apps, partner access, native clients, signatures or legal configuration is engineering work within the relevant clause. It does not shrink the product. Current email/calendar downloads and recorded offer outcomes stay useful as accurately labeled local tools while the connected workflows are built.

## Preserved hiring, privacy and operating guarantees

- **LDG-001 — Exact vacancy and headcount approval.** A vacancy keeps title, location, pay range, hiring manager and headcount. Publishing needs an approval of those exact terms by the assigned, currently active member with the approver role (or the owner); any later change to them makes the approval stale and publishing is refused until it is approved again. Approvers cannot approve their own requests (the owner excepted). **Acceptance evidence:** As manager, request approval, change pay or headcount, and try to publish through the HTTP API; the old approval refuses, while an active independent approver can approve the current terms.

- **LDG-002 — Agreed stages, plan and rubric.** Stages, a versioned job-related rubric (every criterion says what evidence it looks for) and an interview plan exist before approval and publication, and cannot change while the vacancy is published. Each application records the rubric version it is evaluated against. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure stages, two rubric versions and an interview plan through the vacancy commands; publish, attempt edits and verify the application keeps its agreed rubric version.

- **LDG-003 — Accessible applications and privacy receipts.** The public application page works without JavaScript, labels every field, shows the accommodation contact and the current privacy notice, and refuses a submission made against an older notice version, without confirmation, or from another site. Each submission stores the notice version and returns a receipt. Accommodation and voluntary monitoring answers are stored as restricted records. **Acceptance evidence:** Open /jobs and a published job without JavaScript, submit a resume with the current notice, then try stale notices and a foreign origin; receive one valid receipt and keep restricted answers out of reviewer views.

- **LDG-004 — Candidate records, consent and original files.** Staff can add candidates with their source, consent basis, consent note and retention date, link them to a vacancy and attach files; downloads return the original bytes. Files over the stated limit are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Add a referred candidate and original resume through the candidate command, download identical bytes as manager, and refuse an oversized upload and an unauthorized download.

- **LDG-005 — One person and reviewed duplicates.** One candidate can hold applications for several vacancies (never two active ones for the same vacancy). Possible duplicates (same email, phone or name) are listed with their reasons; nothing is merged until a person decides "different people" or "merge", with a reason. Merging refuses until every overlapping active vacancy is resolved explicitly; a successful merge preserves every application and its full history, including closed applications. **Acceptance evidence:** Apply one candidate to two vacancies and review same-email duplicates; refuse overlapping active applications until resolved, merge by an explicit reason and inspect every application and nested history.

- **LDG-006 — Scoped reviewers through every door.** Current interviewers, managers, hiring admins and the owner may review; removing that capability removes old reviewer and panel grants immediately. Interviewers reach only applications they are assigned to (as reviewer or interview panellist), without contact details, restricted documents or restricted answers, and identically through the app's state, search, document downloads, exports and agent proposals. Messages and contact details require current vacancy management. Linking an existing candidate requires pre-existing contact access, so a reviewer cannot widen their grant. Managers' exports and HR handoffs leave restricted metadata and bytes out; owner and hiring-admin exports include them. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as manager, interviewer and agent; compare state, search, document, vacancy and candidate exports; revoke a panel role and verify all doors lose the same access without widening contact grants.

- **LDG-007 — Reasoned stage moves and history.** Moving an application to a stage, or closing it as not progressing or withdrawn, needs a reason and keeps who and when in a readable history. Concurrent moves never both commit from the same state: the history is an unbroken chain. **Acceptance evidence:** Move an application with a reason through the HTTP command, issue concurrent moves and read history; every successful move links to the preceding stage with actor and time.

- **LDG-008 — Interview availability and calendar downloads.** Interviews come from the vacancy's plan with proposed times; panellists record their own availability and the candidate answers through a private link; the hiring team schedules one time and anyone on that specific panel or currently managing that vacancy downloads a standard calendar file. Vacancy management includes the owner and hiring admins; ordinary non-panel reviewers and unrelated managers cannot download it. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a planned panel interview, collect panel availability and candidate private-link choices, schedule and download an ICS as a panel member or manager; unrelated reviewers are refused.

- **LDG-009 — Independent evidence and controlled feedback release.** Each panellist writes their own scorecard against the application's rubric version; every rating carries the interviewer's evidence and unrated criteria stay unanswered; a submitted scorecard is final. Colleagues, including the manager, cannot read a scorecard until the manager releases feedback; releasing before the whole panel submits needs a reason. **Acceptance evidence:** Have two interviewers submit evidenced scorecards, try to read each other before release, release early with a reason and inspect immutable submissions while private source notes stay private.

- **LDG-010 — Exact message approval and unsent downloads.** Candidate messages are drafted, approved as exact text by a hiring manager, and only then downloaded by a current vacancy manager as an `.eml` file marked unsent. The assigned approver must still have the manager role when deciding (the owner excepted). Editing the text makes the approval stale. Each download is recorded. **Acceptance evidence:** Draft and approve exact message text, edit it and refuse the stale download, then reapprove and download an X-Unsent EML; the audit records a download and never delivery.

- **LDG-011 — Exact offer approval and recorded outcome.** An offer records title, pay and start date; its approval packet shows the rubric, stage history, released scorecards, approvals and a plain warning when pay falls outside the approved range. Recording an accepted or declined outcome needs a current approval of the exact terms. Acceptance records the hire and enables an HR handoff export. **Acceptance evidence:** Compile an offer packet with range warning and released feedback, approve exact terms, refuse a changed offer outcome, then record acceptance and download the scoped HR handoff.

- **LDG-012 — Retention, erasure, holds and access exports.** Legal holds (on a candidate or vacancy) block deletion. Deletion needs the candidate's name typed exactly and closed applications; it removes their records, document bytes, the complete merged-duplicate lineage and all related proposal packets (including proposed, rejected and applied packets, their sources, citations, inputs and results) in the same transaction, and removes candidate-linked requests, released candidate holds and audit copies, including export digests and unknown request payloads. The same fenced transaction invalidates all local archive transfers, dropping completed and building export snapshots, import staging, stored archive parts and jobs; refused or stale erasure preserves those transfers. It advances the source erasure epoch so an export prepared against older state cannot publish a late snapshot; future archives keep the safe candidate tombstone. Released holds for their vacancies keep only shared lifecycle metadata; unrelated vacancy history remains. The deletion receipt keeps counts and no free-text deletion reason or personal data, and an old request cannot be completed after erasure. Candidate access/export requests export the requesting admin's authorized records with original document bytes and complete the request with its digest. The primary candidate and merged records remain distinct; nested merged duplicates remain included; independent feedback and private interview notes obey LDG-009 and LDG-013 in exports too. Past-retention records are listed for review. A vacancy's whole history (its terms, approvals, applications, stage history, interviews, the scorecards the requester may read, messages and offers) downloads as one file, scoped to the requester exactly as clause 6 describes. **Acceptance evidence:** Place and release holds, export a candidate request, merge nested duplicates, and erase a closed candidate through HTTP; inspect atomic byte/proposal/archive invalidation, personal-data-free receipt and refusal of stale completion.

- **LDG-013 — Source-bound agent proposals under human approval.** A coding agent works as an agent member acting for one person: it reads exactly that person's view and can only propose. A proposal names the source records and revisions it read, typed changes from its workflow (prepare a vacancy, feedback draft, coordination, talent-pool rediscovery) and the approving person; it is checked against both the proposer's and that person's current source-field and change permissions, hides the whole packet if those grants are lost, flags missing information instead of inventing it, and applies only when that person approves and every source is unchanged. A feedback draft's ratings must cite the interviewer's own notes exactly. Notes and feedback proposal payloads remain private to that interviewer and their agent, even after submitted scorecards are released; a proposal never releases a draft or its source notes. Source reads obey the same scorecard and document rules as screens. Rediscovery needs current permission to contact and shows factual matches without a score. **Acceptance evidence:** As an agent for an interviewer or manager, submit typed proposals, verify exact own-note citations, change a source or revoke authority, and attempt approval; stale or unauthorized proposals refuse and private note packets never become shared scorecards.

- **LDG-014 — Reviewed Workable move-in and undo.** The Workable full-export kit previews a ZIP with counts per area, rows set aside and why, files that match no candidate, resumes named but missing and areas not carried over, writing nothing. A commit must match the reviewed fingerprint and runs through Base import-kit's D1 journal; the same package again creates nothing new; original files are kept unchanged; undo removes only unchanged imported records and retains, with the reason, what people edited or still rely on. Imported people, jobs and history grant no access and no permission to contact. **Acceptance evidence:** Preview, commit, repeat and undo a documented-shape Workable ZIP through the move-in API and page; preview writes nothing, fingerprints bind apply, changed imports survive undo and no access or contact permission is granted.

- **LDG-015 — Whole-installation archive and validated restore.** A whole-installation archive (`rer export`) restores into an empty checkout (`rer import`) with the same hiring records, document bytes and import custody; a non-empty destination or a damaged archive is refused before publication, including recomputed envelopes with missing or malformed blobs, conflicting media types, sizes or digests, or missing candidate/vacancy references on applications and candidate/application references on documents. Dependent interview, feedback, stage, message, offer, receipt and monitoring records must reference an existing application. Candidate merge links must name existing live candidates and form an acyclic lineage; malformed links, self-merges and cycles are refused before publication. Nested native merges and deleted tombstones remain restorable. The archive carries Base's operator identities (never sessions, tokens or dependency keys), so restored members are the same people, the restored owner signs in with an ordinary emailed link, and import batches stay with the owner who ran them. **Acceptance evidence:** Run rer export and rer import into a disposable empty checkout, compare records, original files, people and import custody, and reject nonempty, damaged, semantically invalid or cyclic archives before publication.

- **LDG-016 — Live session and saved authorization.** Every private route checks the current native session, exact origin and the active saved Lodge membership and, for an agent, its active saved principal at the time of the request; deactivating a member removes their access at once; forged actor, author or role fields change nothing; refused requests leave hiring records and audits unchanged. Mutations fence the native session and saved authorization revisions in the transaction, including imports and invitations. Before the first business settings are saved, only the native owner can initialize their membership. **Acceptance evidence:** Call private reads, commands, imports and invitations with signed-out, forged, revoked and foreign-origin requests; assert no refused write or audit change and live transactional authorization fences.

- **LDG-017 — Published rer commands and responsive workspace.** MIT source runs through the maintained `rer` install, setup, dev, build, preview, verify, reset, export, import, deploy --dry-run, describe and check, on TanStack Start, Hono and Cloudflare Workers with D1, its own fonts and theme (`public/rer-theme.css`) and declared dev personas. **Acceptance evidence:** From a fresh checkout run the published install/setup/dev/build/preview/describe/check and archive verbs; use the hiring journey and workspace at 1440 and 390 pixels with no sideways scrolling. CI exercises the full verify path.

## Attract, source and manage candidates

- **ATTR-001 — Branded careers and complete application forms.** An employer publishes a branded careers site on its chosen domain, with searchable location/department/remote openings, USD pay ranges and employment type. Job-specific questions, required attachments and accessible mobile forms support save/resume and receipt recovery. Questions and screening answers are versioned; recruiters review eligibility evidence and decide progression. Required privacy, accommodation and voluntary monitoring controls stay separate from selection evidence. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish two locations and remote roles, open the careers domain at 1440 and 390 pixels, filter jobs, save and resume an application, submit attachments and recover its receipt; keyboard-only and screen-reader journeys preserve labels and errors.

- **ATTR-002 — Named job distribution and source attribution.** Publish, update, close and reconcile approved jobs on Indeed, LinkedIn Jobs and ZipRecruiter, plus Google for Jobs structured listings and a supported free-board feed. Each destination shows its external job ID, actual posting status, last reconciliation and rejection reason; application links and supported apply feeds preserve source/campaign attribution and deduplicate repeated submissions. Paid campaigns require a named buyer approval, visible USD budget and provider cost; access approvals and partner apps are delivery work, never a substitute for these connectors. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect each named destination with authorized employer accounts, publish an approved vacancy, read back its live listing and ingest a sourced application; edit pay, close the job, exceed a campaign budget and disconnect a feed. Inspect correct remote state, attribution and visible errors without duplicate applications.

- **DATA-001 — Configurable candidate profiles and hiring records.** Recruiters configure typed candidate, application and requisition fields, required stage fields, tags, saved views and filters. Search covers permitted profile fields, resume text, jobs and talent pools; custom-field permissions follow LDG-006, and sensitive fields cannot leak into previews or bulk selections. Multiple applications retain separate owners, stage histories and interview evidence. **Acceptance evidence:** Create an employment-type field and a restricted field in settings, capture values through intake and import, filter/search resumes and save a team view; an interviewer cannot discover the restricted value through search or exports.

- **DATA-002 — Individual and bulk resume parsing.** Upload individual files or batches of resumes, preserve original bytes and extract contact, employment, education and skills with source passages and uncertain fields for review. A recruiter confirms corrections and email/name/phone duplicate suggestions before committing people and applications. A parse failure leaves the original downloadable and identifies the affected file; parsing does not infer protected traits or a suitability score. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload PDF, DOCX and text resumes through the intake page and bulk importer, including an unreadable file and duplicate email; review extracted fields against the originals, correct one and commit without overwriting another candidate or losing a file.

- **DATA-003 — Reviewed bulk candidate actions.** Authorized recruiters select candidates from a saved view and preview per-record stage moves, tagging, reviewer assignment, archive, data export and personalized messaging. Current permissions, required reasons, contact preferences and source revisions are checked for every item; each success/refusal is shown and audited. Bulk rejection remains an explicit human decision with exact recipient/text preview; partial failure never silently counts all items as complete. **Acceptance evidence:** Select candidates across two vacancies, preview a bulk move and personalized message, revoke one grant and change one source before apply; inspect item-level outcomes, no unauthorized change and no duplicate delivered message on resubmission.

- **CRM-001 — Searchable consented talent pools.** Authorized admins search retained prior candidates using their recorded words and see factual matching fields, prior applications and current talent-pool permission. No suitability ranking is inferred, expired or erased people are excluded and the recruiter chooses whom to contact. **Acceptance evidence:** Use the talent-pool API as admin and interviewer; find a consented prior applicant by recorded skill, inspect the matching field, and exclude an expired or nonconsenting candidate.

- **CRM-002 — Authorized sourcing and LinkedIn recruiter connection.** Build prospect lists from authorized sourcing providers and recruiter-entered contacts, including LinkedIn Recruiter System Connect with candidate/profile linking, permitted resume/context transfer and application-status synchronization. Preserve source provenance, license restrictions, duplicates, contact basis and opt-outs; show partner authorization and synchronization failures. Passive candidate discovery is an integrated service with disclosed coverage and USD cost, not an invented proprietary network. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect an authorized LinkedIn Recruiter/RSC account and a licensed sourcing service, bring a prospect into a talent pool, reconcile an existing applicant and revoke the provider; inspect provenance, restricted fields, deduplication and no subsequent unauthorized fetch or outreach.

- **CRM-003 — Employee referrals and follow-through.** Employees see eligible jobs in a referral portal, submit a candidate with permission and a resume, track permitted status and receive job-opportunity/referral updates without seeing private feedback or competing applicants. Recruiters resolve duplicates, attribute source of hire and record referral ownership and any employer-approved bonus milestone without running payroll. **Acceptance evidence:** An employee receives an opted-in job alert, refers an existing and a new candidate, and follows the allowed status; recruiter reconciliation preserves one identity and attribution while the employee cannot read scorecards.

- **CRM-004 — Recruiting CRM nurture and candidate rediscovery.** Recruiters segment talent pools, own follow-up tasks and enroll permitted candidates in personalized nurture campaigns for future openings. Every outreach shows why the person is eligible, prior interactions and consent expiry; replies, opt-out, withdrawal or an accepted offer stop stale campaigns. Rediscovery suggests evidenced matches for human selection and never silently advances a candidate. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a pool from past applicants, select factual matches for a new vacancy, preview a nurture sequence and receive a reply and opt-out; verify stopped sends, retained thread history and human-approved application creation.

- **CRM-005 — Scoped recruiting agency collaboration.** Invite an external agency to selected requisitions, accept attributed submissions and resumes, resolve duplicate ownership and expose only the permitted progress/feedback. Agency users lose access immediately on revocation, cannot search the employer database and can reconcile placement/fee milestones in USD without acquiring HR or payroll authority. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite two agencies to disjoint jobs, submit the same consented candidate, resolve ownership and revoke one agency; verify its access through portal, API, search, files and exports is removed and all attribution remains.

- **CAND-001 — Candidate account and self-service.** Candidates securely recover their application receipts, see employer-published progress, update their contact preferences, supply requested information, book or reschedule interviews, withdraw and request access or erasure. Authentication and private-link scope prevent one applicant reaching another; updates do not rewrite submitted evidence or override holds and retention review. **Acceptance evidence:** As an applicant on mobile, recover a receipt, answer a request, change email with verification, withdraw and request data access; try another candidate link and an expired session and inspect no information disclosure or continued sequence.

- **COM-001 — Two-way Gmail and Microsoft 365 candidate email.** Connect Gmail/Google Workspace and Microsoft 365/Outlook with scoped OAuth, shared/team sender permissions, signature and reply threading. Recruiters send approved candidate messages and attachments inside Lodge, receive replies and mailbox-side sends, and see pending, provider-acknowledged, bounced, failed or uncertain outcomes. Provider acceptance is separate from recipient delivery/read signals. Token rotation, revoked access, webhook gaps and reconciliation remain visible; an uncertain send is never blindly replayed. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect each mail provider, send a reviewed candidate thread and attachment, reply from the candidate mailbox and send from the provider UI; confirm one scoped conversation in both systems, revoke OAuth, lose a send response and inspect visible uncertainty without a duplicate send.

- **COM-002 — Templates, personalized bulk mail and sequences.** Own reusable templates, merge fields, sender rules and multi-touch sequences with recruiter-reviewed recipient/text previews. Configure US time zones, business-day waits and contact preferences; send bulk personalized outreach through COM-001. Stop pending steps on reply, opt-out, withdrawal, hire or changed permission. A restart resumes due work exactly once and a failed/uncertain attempt remains owned for reconciliation rather than being marked contacted. **Acceptance evidence:** Preview a two-step personalized campaign with a missing merge field and excluded candidate, approve it, receive a reply before step two and restart the scheduler; inspect no stale follow-up, honest per-recipient outcomes and no duplicate send.

- **COM-003 — Consented SMS and WhatsApp conversations.** Use Twilio for US SMS and WhatsApp Business messaging with employer sender setup, applicable sender registration, opt-in evidence, STOP/HELP handling, channel/time-zone preferences, templates where required and visible USD usage/budgets. Incoming replies join the candidate timeline and stop sequences. Carrier/provider acceptance, delivery failure and uncertainty stay distinct; provider app approval and sender registration are part of shipping the integration. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a registered US sender and authorized WhatsApp account, obtain explicit candidate permission, exchange messages on real handsets and reply STOP; verify no further contact, budget refusal, scoped transcript and visible failed/uncertain carrier outcomes.

- **CAL-001 — Connected self-scheduling and rescheduling.** Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook Calendar expose authorized free/busy, interviewer work hours, buffers, locations and time zones without disclosing private event details. Candidates choose a live conflict-checked slot; Lodge books panel invitations, receives RSVP and calendar-side changes, reschedules or cancels the same interview and sends correct notices. Recheck availability before commit, hold races safely and keep provider errors or uncertain bookings unresolved until readback. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect Google and Outlook calendars, book a mixed-provider panel from a candidate link across a US daylight-saving transition, introduce a competing booking and reschedule from both calendar and candidate UI; inspect one current event per participant with correct notices and unresolved failures.

- **CAL-002 — Interviewer pools and complex interview days.** Coordinate multi-session interview plans using qualified interviewer pools, required/optional participants, load caps, working hours, breaks and room/resource availability. Propose feasible panels and replacements, explain conflicts and preserve independent scorecard assignments. The recruiter approves a changed interview plan or substitute before candidate commitments change. **Acceptance evidence:** Plan a three-session interview day across two time zones and a room, exceed one interviewer cap and remove a panellist; review feasible alternatives and approve a substitute before CAL-001 updates events.

- **CAL-003 — Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams interviews.** Create and reconcile video meetings through Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams on the connected organizer account. The same usable join details appear in candidate notices, panel calendar events and the authorized interview kit; rescheduling and cancellation update or revoke the correct meeting, and licensing, permission and creation failures stay visible. **Acceptance evidence:** Book a real interview on each video service, open its join link as candidate and panellist, reschedule and cancel; revoke organizer permission and verify the booking is visibly incomplete rather than carrying a fabricated link.

- **AUTO-001 — Owner-editable stage workflows and approval chains.** Recruiting admins configure versioned triggers and conditions by vacancy, stage, location, employment type and USD compensation. Actions include assigned tasks, approved message templates, booking links, questionnaires and employer-chosen assessments, plus sequential/parallel headcount and offer approvers with delegation and escalation. Preview recipients, scopes and required decisions in a sandbox; edits invalidate obsolete approvals and do not retroactively run against history or decide suitability. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a screening stage with questionnaire, booking link and an above-budget two-person offer chain; preview, activate and move an application through HTTP/UI. Change terms mid-approval and restart; stale approval refuses and each permitted action executes once.

- **AUTO-002 — Persistent hiring reminders and escalation.** Configure candidate/panel scheduling reminders, upcoming-interview reminders and repeated missing-scorecard requests with a named owner, next due time, business hours, maximum cadence and manager escalation. A response, submission, cancellation, withdrawal, reassignment or contact revocation stops obsolete work. Show queued, attempted, acknowledged, failed and uncertain outcomes across restart; creating a task never counts as contacting its recipient. **Acceptance evidence:** Leave availability and a completed interview scorecard outstanding, observe repeated delivered reminders and an escalation, submit the missing items and restart between due times; no stale reminder or duplicate attempt survives.

- **OFFER-001 — Reusable US offer documents and compensation packets.** Create versioned offer templates and merge approved title, location, employment type, annual/hourly USD pay, start date, bonus/equity and employer-reviewed contingencies. Preview candidate-specific documents and route compensation exceptions to the right approvers before sending. The exact document/terms hash binds approvals and signature envelope; material changes require reapproval and a replacement document. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare hourly and salaried offers from reviewed templates, insert a missing field and an out-of-range amount, obtain approvals and change compensation afterward; inspect refused stale send and the correct regenerated packet.

- **OFFER-002 — DocuSign execution and signed-offer custody.** Send the approved offer through DocuSign, track sent/viewed/signed/declined/expired/voided status by provider readback, and retain the signed original, envelope identifiers and completion evidence with access and retention controls. Candidate acceptance is established by the required signature, not an email approval or manually recorded status. Duplicate callbacks, uncertain submission, revisions and revoked access cannot create a second active offer or a false signature. **Acceptance evidence:** Send an approved real DocuSign envelope, sign as candidate, reconcile its completed artifact and replay a callback; test decline, expiry, void and a lost send response. Only read-back completion unlocks the accepted-hire handoff.

- **HIRE-001 — Verified Willow, BambooHR and ADP handoff.** After the required signed acceptance and employer-approved contingencies, preview employee creation in [Willow](https://github.com/just-every/run-willow), BambooHR or ADP Workforce Now. Map identity, role, manager, location, employment type, approved USD pay, start date and signed documents. A named person authorizes creation; a stable handoff key, destination identity/terms readback and exception owner prove completion, and retries cannot create a second employee. Lodge retains recruiting history while the HRIS owns onboarding, I-9, W-2/1099 classification records, benefits and payroll. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a signed hire into each named HRIS, map a custom field, preview and authorize creation; interrupt the response, reconcile by handoff key and compare destination identity/terms/documents. A missing field or mismatched employee stays an owned exception, never a successful handoff.

- **HIRE-002 — Checkr consent, results and employer decisions.** Order the employer-approved Checkr package with candidate authorization, track invitation and check status, and show authorized report links/status with strict access and retention. Provider failures, identity ambiguity and pending results remain visible. The employer decides pre-adverse/adverse action using its reviewed Checkr process, supplies required notices and waits, and records evidence; Lodge neither acts as a reporting agency nor makes an adverse employment decision. **Acceptance evidence:** With authorized Checkr test/production access, order a consented package from a candidate profile, reconcile pending/completed/disputed status and a failure; an interviewer cannot read report details and no result automatically rejects or advances the candidate.

- **REPORT-001 — Recruiting analytics and customizable reporting.** Recruiters build filtered dashboards for opening/headcount status, time-to-fill, time-to-hire, stage age/conversion, scheduling delay, feedback completion, source of hire, offer acceptance, sourcing activity and cost per hire in USD. Every metric discloses event definitions, denominator, cohort/time-zone filters and imported-history treatment, and drills to authorized underlying records. Export reports and compare source quality without inventing suitability or protected-trait inferences. **Acceptance evidence:** Use a cohort with two openings, withdrawals, reopened stages and imported history; build a funnel and source/time-to-fill report through the UI, drill to records and export. Compare recomputed event-based totals and verify unauthorized candidates are absent.

- **REPORT-002 — US EEO and OFCCP recruiting records.** Employers configure reviewed voluntary US EEO self-identification and applicable OFCCP disability/veteran questionnaires separately from selection. Preserve form/notice version, decline-to-answer, consent, job/requisition disposition reasons, applicant-flow records and legally reviewed retention/holds. Authorized compliance users export aggregate EEO and OFCCP applicant-flow reports with documented definitions and restricted individual records; managers, interviewers, agents and suitability decisions cannot use monitoring answers. Applicability and filing remain the employer/legal adviser responsibility. **Acceptance evidence:** Apply with voluntary responses and prefer-not-to-answer choices, record a human disposition, and export employer-reviewed EEO/OFCCP reports as compliance admin; compare source rows and verify ordinary reviewers, search, scoring and agents never see the protected answers.

- **REPORT-003 — Scheduled private recruiting reports.** Deliver recipient-scoped dashboards on an employer-selected schedule by email or Slack, with time-zone-aware cadence, metric definitions, period and authenticated drill links. Recheck permissions at send and download, redact private data, expose delivery outcomes and suspend jobs when recipients or connectors are revoked. **Acceptance evidence:** Schedule a weekly source/funnel report to email and Slack, revoke one recipient before the due time and restart; eligible people receive the correct period once and the revoked person cannot receive or open restricted data.

- **REPORT-004 — Candidate experience surveys.** Send opt-in surveys after employer-selected stages and closure, keep response rates and small-sample limits visible, and connect authorized feedback to stage/scheduling delays for process improvement. Anonymous responses are not deanonymized in candidate views, contact opt-outs and retention apply and survey answers never become suitability scores. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a post-interview and withdrawal survey, respond anonymously and by consented identity, then compare permitted aggregate results and response denominator; decline contact and verify no further survey or identification leak.

- **COLLAB-001 — Slack and Teams hiring collaboration.** Connect Slack and Microsoft Teams for scoped hiring notifications, interview kits, referral opportunities and actionable requisition/offer or screening-booking approvals. Automatically create and archive permitted candidate interview channels when configured. Each action links to the exact current packet and checks live identity and authority before applying; private scorecards/notes and monitoring answers are excluded. Channel access drift, failed posts and stale decisions stay visible. **Acceptance evidence:** Install each workspace app, create an interview channel, approve a screening booking and offer from chat, change the offer first and revoke a reviewer; stale/revoked actions refuse, feedback blinding holds and closing the process archives only its owned channel.

- **INT-001 — Public recruiting API, webhooks and Zapier.** Publish versioned authenticated APIs for permitted candidates, requisitions, applications, interviews, decisions, offers, reports and migration. Scoped app credentials, signed webhook events with stable IDs, delivery history, replay controls and a Zapier app support extensions without bypassing human decisions, consent or approval fences. Imported history does not replay live notifications; API and UI permissions agree. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a scoped integration and a Zapier workflow, read and update an authorized application, consume a signed stage event and replay it; revoke the token and verify immediate denial, no duplicate downstream action and no access to another job.

- **INT-002 — Connector administration and honest execution.** Admins install named provider apps, grant minimum scopes, choose sender/organizer and data mappings, test access, rotate credentials, monitor synchronization and disconnect. Secrets stay installation-local and outside exports, model context and logs. Operations have stable identities and visible pending/acknowledged/failed/uncertain states, safe rate-limit scheduling and reconciliation. Reconnection cannot replay withdrawn candidate contact, past imports or an uncertain send; provider receipts never imply a human read or signed/created outcome without the relevant readback. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect, rotate and revoke mail/calendar/DocuSign/HRIS apps, restart during a lost response, inject provider rate limits and inspect connection health plus archives/logs; no leaked credential, replayed stale work or fabricated completion appears.

- **MOB-001 — Mobile recruiting and native apps.** Recruiters and interviewers use responsive web and native iOS/Android apps for candidate search, interview kits, private scorecards, tasks and authorized approvals; candidates apply, schedule and sign on mobile. ## Honest boundaries and product ownership

Lodge owns the full recruiting job, including named integrations and follow-through. [Willow](https://github.com/just-every/run-willow) or the buyer's BambooHR/ADP owns employee HR, onboarding, benefits, I-9, payroll and tax records after HIRE-001's verified handoff. Lodge never replaces those products or absorbs an unowned category.

Email, calendar, job-board distribution, licensed sourcing, telephony, video, e-signature and background-report infrastructure use the named provider connections. Provider subscriptions, app/partner approval, sender registration, recording permissions and service costs must be shown during setup and delivered as part of the integration work. No provider account means that operation is visibly unconnected, not simulated or silently successful. Lodge remains MIT and self-hostable through LDG-017; installation ownership does not grant provider-network rights.

Humans decide who advances, who is rejected, whom to hire, compensation, legal terms and adverse actions. Lodge provides no autonomous suitability score, protected-trait inference or AI interviewer. Consented capture/notes are NOTE-001's supported workflow. An employer and its counsel determine applicable US pay-transparency, recording, retention, EEO/OFCCP and background-check obligations; configurable evidence/reporting supports that work without claiming universal legal compliance. Downloaded archives and already delivered third-party copies require the employer's separate custody/erasure process.

The existing Base-backed runtime, authentication, archive/import-kit and erasure guarantees are preserved in LDG-012, LDG-014–017. This contract makes no replacement of Base authority, no new product merger and no claim that an unbuilt connection is delivered. Lodge's owner-adopted name dates to 10 October 2026; legal name clearance remains required before public branding.
