# Lodge target product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Lodge is a complete SMB applicant tracking and recruiting product: attract and source applicants, communicate continuously, coordinate interviews, collect independent evidence, obtain signed offers and verify handoff into HR. A US internal recruiter or operations lead at a 20–200 employee employer can switch from the named ATS and recruiting tools below. USD is the default for pay, budgets and costs; US time zones, daylight saving and employer-reviewed state/location requirements are explicit.

This contract describes the product being built towards. The status table separates current local implementation from missing execution; difficulty obtaining provider apps, partner access, native clients, signatures or legal configuration is engineering work within the relevant clause. It does not shrink the product. Current email/calendar downloads and recorded offer outcomes stay useful as accurately labeled local tools while the connected workflows are built.

## Preserved hiring, privacy and operating guarantees

- **LDG-001 — Exact vacancy and headcount approval.** A vacancy keeps title, location, pay range, hiring manager and headcount. Publishing needs an approval of those exact terms by the assigned, currently active member with the approver role (or the owner); any later change to them makes the approval stale and publishing is refused until it is approved again. Approvers cannot approve their own requests (the owner excepted). **Acceptance evidence:** As manager, request approval, change pay or headcount, and try to publish through the HTTP API; the old approval refuses, while an active independent approver can approve the current terms.

- **LDG-002 — Agreed stages, plan and rubric.** Stages, a versioned job-related rubric (every criterion says what evidence it looks for) and an interview plan exist before approval and publication, and cannot change while the vacancy is published. Each application records the rubric version it is evaluated against. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure stages, two rubric versions and an interview plan through the vacancy commands; publish, attempt edits and verify the application keeps its agreed rubric version.

- **LDG-003 — Accessible applications and privacy receipts.** The public application page works without JavaScript, labels every field, shows the accommodation contact and the current privacy notice, and refuses a submission made against an older notice version, without confirmation, or from another site. Each submission stores the notice version and returns a receipt whose time reads in the business's own time zone. Accommodation and voluntary monitoring answers are stored as restricted records. **Acceptance evidence:** Open /jobs and a published job without JavaScript, submit a resume with the current notice, then try stale notices and a foreign origin; receive one valid receipt and keep restricted answers out of reviewer views.

- **LDG-004 — Candidate records, consent and original files.** Staff can add candidates with their source, consent basis, consent note and retention date, link them to a vacancy and attach files; downloads return the original bytes. Files over the stated limit are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Add a referred candidate and original resume through the candidate command, download identical bytes as manager, and refuse an oversized upload and an unauthorized download.

- **LDG-005 — One person and reviewed duplicates.** One candidate can hold applications for several vacancies (never two active ones for the same vacancy). Possible duplicates (same email, phone or name) are listed with their reasons; nothing is merged until a person decides "different people" or "merge", with a reason. Merging refuses until every overlapping active vacancy is resolved explicitly; a successful merge preserves every application and its full history, including closed applications. **Acceptance evidence:** Apply one candidate to two vacancies and review same-email duplicates; refuse overlapping active applications until resolved, merge by an explicit reason and inspect every application and nested history.

- **LDG-006 — Scoped reviewers through every door.** Current interviewers, managers, hiring admins and the owner may review; removing that capability removes old reviewer and panel grants immediately. Interviewers reach only applications they are assigned to (as reviewer or interview panellist), without contact details, restricted documents or restricted answers, and identically through the app's state, search, document downloads, exports and agent proposals. Messages and contact details require current vacancy management. Linking an existing candidate requires pre-existing contact access, so a reviewer cannot widen their grant. Managers' exports and HR handoffs leave restricted metadata and bytes out; owner and hiring-admin exports include them. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as manager, interviewer and agent; compare state, search, document, vacancy and candidate exports; revoke a panel role and verify all doors lose the same access without widening contact grants.

- **LDG-007 — Reasoned stage moves and history.** Moving an application to a stage, or closing it as not progressing or withdrawn, needs a reason and keeps who and when in a readable history. Concurrent moves never both commit from the same state: the history is an unbroken chain. **Acceptance evidence:** Move an application with a reason through the HTTP command, issue concurrent moves and read history; every successful move links to the preceding stage with actor and time.

- **LDG-008 — Interview availability and calendar downloads.** Interviews come from the vacancy's plan with proposed times; panellists record their own availability and the candidate answers through a private link; the hiring team schedules one time and anyone on that specific panel or currently managing that vacancy downloads a standard calendar file. Vacancy management includes the owner and hiring admins; ordinary non-panel reviewers and unrelated managers cannot download it. A panellist can tell the hiring team they cannot attend, and another interviewer can offer to cover with the time they are free, each in their own words; neither changes the interview. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a planned panel interview, collect panel availability and candidate private-link choices, schedule and download an ICS as a panel member or manager; unrelated reviewers are refused.

- **LDG-009 — Independent evidence and controlled feedback release.** Each panellist writes their own scorecard against the application's rubric version; every rating carries the interviewer's evidence and unrated criteria stay unanswered; a submitted scorecard is final. Colleagues, including the manager, cannot read a scorecard until the manager releases feedback; releasing before the whole panel submits needs a reason. **Acceptance evidence:** Have two interviewers submit evidenced scorecards, try to read each other before release, release early with a reason and inspect immutable submissions while private source notes stay private.

- **LDG-010 — Exact message approval and unsent downloads.** Candidate messages are drafted, approved as exact text by a hiring manager, and only then downloaded by a current vacancy manager as an `.eml` file marked unsent. The assigned approver must still have the manager role when deciding (the owner excepted). Editing the text makes the approval stale. Each download is recorded. **Acceptance evidence:** Draft and approve exact message text, edit it and refuse the stale download, then reapprove and download an X-Unsent EML; the audit records a download and never delivery.

- **LDG-011 — Exact offer approval and recorded outcome.** An offer records title, pay and start date; its approval packet shows the rubric, stage history, released scorecards, approvals and a plain warning when pay falls outside the approved range. Recording an accepted or declined outcome needs a current approval of the exact terms. Acceptance records the hire and enables an HR handoff export. **Acceptance evidence:** Compile an offer packet with range warning and released feedback, approve exact terms, refuse a changed offer outcome, then record acceptance and download the scoped HR handoff.

- **LDG-012 — Retention, erasure, holds and access exports.** Legal holds (on a candidate or vacancy) block deletion. Deletion needs the candidate's name typed exactly and closed applications; it removes their records, document bytes, the complete merged-duplicate lineage and all related proposal packets (including proposed, rejected and applied packets, their sources, citations, inputs and results) in the same transaction, and removes candidate-linked requests, released candidate holds and audit copies, including export digests and unknown request payloads. The same fenced transaction invalidates all local archive transfers, dropping completed and building export snapshots, import staging, stored archive parts and jobs; refused or stale erasure preserves those transfers. It advances the source erasure epoch so an export prepared against older state cannot publish a late snapshot; future archives keep the safe candidate tombstone. Released holds for their vacancies keep only shared lifecycle metadata; unrelated vacancy history remains. The deletion receipt keeps counts and no free-text deletion reason or personal data, and an old request cannot be completed after erasure. Candidate access/export requests export the requesting admin's authorized records with original document bytes and complete the request with its digest. The primary candidate and merged records remain distinct; nested merged duplicates remain included; independent feedback and private interview notes obey LDG-009 and LDG-013 in exports too. Past-retention records are listed for review. A vacancy's whole history (its terms, approvals, applications, stage history, interviews, the scorecards the requester may read, messages and offers) downloads as one file, scoped to the requester exactly as clause 6 describes. **Acceptance evidence:** Place and release holds, export a candidate request, merge nested duplicates, and erase a closed candidate through HTTP; inspect atomic byte/proposal/archive invalidation, personal-data-free receipt and refusal of stale completion.

- **LDG-013 — Source-bound agent proposals under human approval.** A coding agent works as an agent member acting for one person: it reads exactly that person's view and can only propose. A proposal names the source records and revisions it read, typed changes from its workflow (prepare a vacancy, feedback draft, coordination, talent-pool rediscovery) and the approving person; it is checked against both the proposer's and that person's current source-field and change permissions, hides the whole packet if those grants are lost, flags missing information instead of inventing it, and applies only when that person approves and every source is unchanged. A feedback draft's ratings must cite the interviewer's own notes exactly. Notes and feedback proposal payloads remain private to that interviewer and their agent, even after submitted scorecards are released; a proposal never releases a draft or its source notes. Source reads obey the same scorecard and document rules as screens. Rediscovery needs current permission to contact and shows factual matches without a score. **Acceptance evidence:** As an agent for an interviewer or manager, submit typed proposals, verify exact own-note citations, change a source or revoke authority, and attempt approval; stale or unauthorized proposals refuse and private note packets never become shared scorecards.

- **LDG-014 — Reviewed Workable move-in and undo.** The Workable full-export kit previews a ZIP with counts per area, rows set aside and why, files that match no candidate, resumes named but missing and areas not carried over, writing nothing. A commit must match the reviewed fingerprint and runs through Base import-kit's D1 journal; the same package again creates nothing new; original files are kept unchanged; undo removes only unchanged imported records and retains, with the reason, what people edited or still rely on. Imported people, jobs and history grant no access and no permission to contact. Carrying Workable applications that are still active into live pipeline is MIG-008's separate, recruiter-approved active-application cutover; importing alone never does it. **Acceptance evidence:** Preview, commit, repeat and undo a documented-shape Workable ZIP through the move-in API and page; preview writes nothing, fingerprints bind apply, changed imports survive undo and no access or contact permission is granted.

- **LDG-015 — Whole-installation archive and validated restore.** A whole-installation archive (`rer export`) restores into an empty checkout (`rer import`) with the same hiring records, document bytes and import custody; a non-empty destination or a damaged archive is refused before publication, including recomputed envelopes with missing or malformed blobs, conflicting media types, sizes or digests, or missing candidate/vacancy references on applications and candidate/application references on documents. Dependent interview, feedback, stage, message, offer, receipt and monitoring records must reference an existing application. Candidate merge links must name existing live candidates and form an acyclic lineage; malformed links, self-merges and cycles are refused before publication. Nested native merges and deleted tombstones remain restorable. The archive carries Base's operator identities (never sessions, tokens or dependency keys), so restored members are the same people, the restored owner signs in with an ordinary emailed link, and import batches stay with the owner who ran them. **Acceptance evidence:** Run rer export and rer import into a disposable empty checkout, compare records, original files, people and import custody, and reject nonempty, damaged, semantically invalid or cyclic archives before publication.

- **LDG-016 — Live session and saved authorization.** Every private route checks the current native session, exact origin and the active saved Lodge membership and, for an agent, its active saved principal at the time of the request; deactivating a member removes their access at once; forged actor, author or role fields change nothing; refused requests leave hiring records and audits unchanged. Mutations fence the native session and saved authorization revisions in the transaction, including imports and invitations. Before the first business settings are saved, only the native owner can initialize their membership. **Acceptance evidence:** Call private reads, commands, imports and invitations with signed-out, forged, revoked and foreign-origin requests; assert no refused write or audit change and live transactional authorization fences.

- **LDG-017 — Published rer commands and responsive workspace.** MIT source runs through the maintained `rer` install, setup, dev, build, preview, verify, reset, export, import, deploy --dry-run, describe and check, on TanStack Start, Hono and Cloudflare Workers with D1, its own fonts and theme (`public/rer-theme.css`) and declared dev personas. **Acceptance evidence:** From a fresh checkout run the published install/setup/dev/build/preview/describe/check and archive verbs; use the hiring journey and workspace at 1440 and 390 pixels with no sideways scrolling. CI exercises the full verify path.

## Target clauses

### Jobs, openings and hiring teams

- **REQ-001 — Openings, opening IDs and close reasons.** A job carries one opening per approved headcount, each with an employer-defined or generated opening ID, target start date, status (open, filled, closed) and close reason. An accepted offer from any OFFER-002–OFFER-006 path fills a named opening; closing an opening without a hire needs a reason; adding an opening needs LDG-001 headcount approval. Openings feed REPORT-001 time-to-fill per opening and HIRE-001–HIRE-005 position mapping. Exact semantic: openings are created when LDG-001 approves the job's headcount, one per approved head, and every opening stays listed as requested, open, filled, closed or refused. A generated opening ID reads `LDG-<job reference>-<nn>`; an employer-defined one uses letters, digits, dots, dashes and underscores (at most 40) and is unique across the business ignoring case; the target start date defaults to the kickoff's (REQ-005) and stays editable while the opening is open or requested. Today's path to a hire is the LDG-011 recorded acceptance: the offer names an open opening as part of its approved terms, and on a job with openings an offer that names none cannot be accepted; OFFER-002–OFFER-006 inherit naming the opening before they ship. Jobs approved before openings existed have none until their next headcount approval, and their accepted offers fill none. The headcount cannot drop below the approved openings; an opening is closed with a reason instead. Adding an opening raises the headcount by one and requests LDG-001 approval: the opening is requested and cannot be offered until that approval, when it opens with its own open date; a refusal marks it refused, returns the headcount to the approved openings and leaves the earlier approval in force. Each opening keeps its open, filled or closed time, and the openings report (`GET /api/lodge/reports/openings`, REPORT-001's first slice) gives each filled opening's time to fill in whole calendar days, business time zone, from its own open date; the HR handoff names the filled opening for HIRE-001–HIRE-005 position mapping, which inherit it before they ship; erasure keeps a filled opening's dates without the erased offer or application. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a job with headcount 3 and see three openings with IDs; fill one through an accepted offer, close one as "budget cut" with a reason and request a fourth opening, which needs approval before it opens; REPORT-001 time-to-fill reports each opening from its own open date.

- **REQ-002 — Job templates and cloning.** Admins keep job templates holding stages, interview plan and kits (KIT-001), rubric, screening questions (SCREEN-001), application rules (SCREEN-002), hiring-team roles (REQ-003), offer template (OFFER-001) and approval chain (AUTO-001). A new job starts from a template or by cloning an existing job and then follows LDG-001 approval. Template edits never change existing jobs, and a clone never copies candidates, approvals or openings. The clause is met when every part of a job's setup that exists today travels this way: stages, the interview plan with its kits, the rubric, screening questions, application rules and hiring-team roles; OFFER-001 offer templates and AUTO-001 approval chains join templates and clones before they ship. Exact semantic: the owner or a hiring admin creates a template from a job's setup (or from stages and criteria) and edits it; each rubric change is the template's next rubric version and the template keeps every version. A job started from a template copies the template as it reads at that moment: its rubric starts at version 1, its rules start at version 1 with no automatic-rejection approval (SCREEN-002), its team is checked as REQ-003 requires that day, and its own terms (title, location, pay, headcount, hiring manager) come with the request. A clone copies another job's terms, with an optional new title, location, office or department, and its current setup the same way, and never its candidates, applications, approvals, openings, kickoff, published page or rule approvals; a confidential job's clone stays confidential to the same named people (OPS-005). Admins and hiring managers start jobs from templates; whoever runs a job may clone it for its own hiring manager. Either way the new job is a draft that needs its own LDG-001 approval before it publishes. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a "Warehouse Associate" template and two location jobs from it; change the template rubric and confirm both jobs keep theirs; clone one job and confirm no candidates, approvals or filled openings carry over and the clone needs its own approval.

- **REQ-003 — Per-job hiring team roles.** Each job names its hiring team: recruiters, coordinators, hiring managers, sourcers and interviewers, each with a permission level (OPS-004). Notifications (TEAM-003), HOME-001 items, AUTO-002 escalation owners, REVIEW-001 queues and COORD-001 default owners follow these roles. Changing the team changes access immediately through every door, as LDG-006 requires. Exact semantic (ct, 19 Oct): the owner or a hiring admin sets the team of a live job, and the job's own hiring manager is always one of its hiring managers. Recruiters, coordinators and hiring managers run the job like its hiring manager through every door, except approving its terms and offers, which stays with the approver role (LDG-001, LDG-011). Sourcers see the job and add candidates to it; so that nobody is sourced twice they also see the job's candidate list as names and current stage (ct, 19 Oct 05:16Z: active applications by stage name, closed ones by their outcome; spam, erased and private candidates they are not cleared for left out), and read no application content, contact details, scorecard, note or offer. Interviewers are the job's interview pool and, as LDG-006 requires, see an application only when assigned to review it or on its panel. On a confidential job (OPS-005) the hiring team counts as named. Each role still needs its Lodge role to be named: recruiters, coordinators and hiring managers hold the manager or admin role, interviewers the interviewer, manager or admin role, and sourcers any Lodge role; an OPS-004 level assigned to a team role on the job then decides what that role may do there. Someone taken off the team loses access at once, and their open follow-ups and tasks on the job's applications (TASK-001) go to whoever took their place in the same role in that change, else the job's first recruiter, else its own hiring manager. Today's in-app notifications follow the roles: a due reminder reaches its owner, and a scorecard correction after release (SCORE-002) reaches every hiring manager on the job; TEAM-003 subscriptions, HOME-001 items, AUTO-002 escalation owners, REVIEW-001 queues and COORD-001 default owners read the same team before they ship. **Acceptance evidence:** Set a recruiter, a coordinator and two hiring managers on a job; each sees and is notified only about that job's items; swap the coordinator and confirm pending reminders move to the new coordinator while the old one loses access at once through the app, API, search and export.

- **REQ-004 — Departments and offices.** Admins define a department hierarchy and an office (US location) list. Jobs, openings and members attach to them; permissions can be granted by department or office (OPS-004); the careers page filters by them (ATTR-001); reports roll up by them (REPORT-001). Renaming keeps history and deleting a node still in use is refused. Exact semantic: the owner or a hiring admin keeps departments (at most six levels deep, names unique among siblings ignoring case, never inside themselves) and offices (a named US location such as "Denver, CO" or "Remote (US)", typed by the admin; Lodge does not geocode or verify it). A job attaches to one department and one office, and its department and location text are filled from them when it is saved (that text is part of the LDG-001 approved terms, so a later rename leaves approved jobs unchanged while the department keeps its earlier names); an opening may name its own office; a member has a home department and office. Grants apply to managers only (ct, 19 Oct), and an OPS-004 level can go with each grant: a manager granted a department (with its sub-departments) or an office runs every job in it, except confidential jobs they are not cleared for (OPS-005), through every door; admins stay business-wide. The careers page at /jobs filters by department (with sub-departments) and by office, counting a job's open openings' offices; a confidential job listed under a neutral title is never in a department filter. Deleting a department or office that a job, an opening, a member, a grant or a sub-department still uses is refused. The openings report filters its jobs, openings and summed funnel by department (with sub-departments) and office. ATTR-001's custom-domain careers site, REPORT-001's other reports and OPS-004 levels inherit these departments and offices before they ship. **Acceptance evidence:** Create Operations › Warehouse and offices Denver and Remote (US), attach jobs, filter the careers page and a REPORT-001 funnel by them, grant a manager Operations only and confirm they cannot find an Engineering job through search, API or export; deleting Denver while a job uses it is refused.

- **REQ-005 — Hiring kickoff and job intake.** A job can start from a kickoff form the hiring manager completes: role purpose, must-have and nice-to-have criteria, proposed interview plan and interviewers, USD pay range request, headcount and target start date. The recruiter turns it into the job draft with rubric and plan; the kickoff stays linked, and a change to its pay or headcount after approval makes the LDG-001 approval stale. **Acceptance evidence:** As hiring manager complete a kickoff, as recruiter create the job from it and get it approved; change the requested pay range afterward and confirm the approval is stale and publishing refuses until re-approved.

### Attract, source and manage candidates

- **ATTR-001 — Branded careers and complete application forms.** An employer publishes a branded careers site on its chosen domain. An admin connects a custom domain (for example careers.example.com) by adding the DNS record Lodge shows; Lodge verifies the record and serves the site over a valid TLS certificate that it renews, and the installation's own /jobs stays available. The employer's logo, brand colors (refused when text contrast falls below WCAG AA) and intro text appear on every careers, job and application page. The site lists searchable location/department/remote openings with USD pay ranges and employment type. Job-specific questions, required attachments and accessible mobile forms support save/resume and receipt recovery. Questions and screening answers are versioned; recruiters review eligibility evidence and decide progression. Required privacy, accommodation and voluntary monitoring controls stay separate from selection evidence. The same jobs and application form reach the employer's existing website as an embed (ATTR-008) and through the public job-board API (ATTR-009); the logo, brand colors and receipt recovery shown here also appear in the ATTR-008 frame. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect careers.<test domain> through the DNS record shown and load it over HTTPS with a valid certificate; an unverified domain is refused. Upload a logo and set brand colors: both appear on the careers list, a job and its application page, and a color pair below AA contrast is refused. Publish two locations and remote roles, open the careers domain at 1440 and 390 pixels, filter jobs, save and resume an application, submit attachments and recover its receipt; keyboard-only and screen-reader journeys preserve labels and errors; the logo, colors and receipt recovery also appear in the ATTR-008 embed.

- **ATTR-002 — Named job distribution and source attribution.** Publish, update, close and reconcile approved jobs on Indeed, LinkedIn Jobs and ZipRecruiter, plus Google for Jobs structured listings; the free LinkedIn Limited Listings and Indeed/Glassdoor feeds are ATTR-003 and Handshake, Wellfound, Built In and Dice are ATTR-006. Each destination shows its external job ID, actual posting status, last reconciliation and rejection reason. Applications come back either by click-through to the hosted application, carrying a per-board source code, or in-board through Indeed Apply (ATTR-010) and LinkedIn Apply Connect (ATTR-011), which carry the job's screening questions; every path preserves source/campaign attribution and deduplicates repeated submissions. Paid campaigns require a named buyer approval, visible USD budget and provider cost; access approvals and partner apps are delivery work, never a substitute for these connectors. Each board or campaign it brings applications from joins the CRM-008 source list automatically (with its type), as the application page's source does today, and credit follows CRM-008. Internal-only jobs (ATTR-005) never reach it. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect Indeed, LinkedIn Jobs and ZipRecruiter with authorized employer accounts and validate the Google for Jobs JobPosting markup with Google's Rich Results Test; publish an approved vacancy, read back its live listing on each, ingest one click-through application from ZipRecruiter and one Indeed Apply application (ATTR-010); edit pay, close the job, exceed a campaign budget and disconnect a feed. Inspect correct remote state, attribution and visible errors without duplicate applications. Each board's source appears on the CRM-008 source list without an admin adding it. An internal-only job (ATTR-005) never appears. Each ATTR-012 job post with one of these boards among its destinations is published, updated, paused and closed there as its own listing, and applications from it record the post (the ATTR-012 journey's Indeed post is proven here).

- **ATTR-003 — Named free job-board feeds.** Approved jobs marked for free distribution publish through LinkedIn Limited Listings (LinkedIn's XML job feed for the employer's LinkedIn Company Page) and Indeed organic listings (Indeed's XML feed or Job Sync API, which Indeed syndicates to Glassdoor). Each entry carries the approved title, location, USD pay range (ATTR-004), employment type and an apply URL with a per-board source code. Publish, edit and close reach the feed at once and the board within its documented refresh cycle; where the board exposes listing status Lodge reads it back, and otherwise shows "feed served, listing not confirmed" rather than "live". Unpublished, confidential (OPS-005) and internal-only (ATTR-005) jobs never enter a feed. Each board or campaign it brings applications from joins the CRM-008 source list automatically (with its type), as the application page's source does today, and credit follows CRM-008. Internal-only jobs (ATTR-005) never reach it. **Acceptance evidence:** Mark two approved jobs for free distribution; validate the served LinkedIn and Indeed feeds against each board's published schema; confirm the listings on the employer's LinkedIn Company Page and on Indeed and Glassdoor; apply through each and see the source recorded as "LinkedIn Limited Listing" or "Indeed (organic)"; close one job and confirm it leaves both feeds and boards, and that a confidential job never appears. Each board's source appears on the CRM-008 source list without an admin adding it. An internal-only job (ATTR-005) never appears. Each ATTR-012 job post with a free feed among its destinations is its own feed entry; pausing or closing the post removes it.

- **ATTR-004 — US pay-transparency postings and salary-history guards.** Every published job shows its approved good-faith USD pay range and period, with per-location ranges when one job posts to several US locations, and the employer's benefits and bonus description where the employer's counsel configures a state or city rule (for example Colorado, California, Washington, New York or Illinois). Publishing to a location whose rule needs a range or benefits text is refused until it is present; the careers page, every feed (ATTR-002, ATTR-003, ATTR-006) and the ATTR-009 board API (its `pay` array, one entry per approved location, and its `payTransparency` text) carry the same text. Application forms refuse salary-history questions for locations the employer flags as salary-history-ban jurisdictions. Rules carry a dated legal review; Lodge does not claim universal compliance. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure Colorado and New York City rules, create a job posted to both with one location missing its range and see publishing refused; add the range, publish and read it on the careers page, the ATTR-003 feed and the ATTR-009 board API (one `pay` entry per location); add a "current salary" question to the New York City job and see it refused. Each ATTR-012 job post carries the ranges of its own locations.

- **ATTR-005 — Internal job board and internal applicants.** Signed-in employees see an internal job board of public and internal-only jobs and apply with their member identity, not the public form; the application is tagged internal with source "Internal". The applicant's current manager is notified only when employer policy says so; internal applications are visible only to the job's hiring team and admins; internal-only jobs never reach the public careers page, the ATTR-008 embed, the ATTR-009 board API (list, detail, department and office counts, submit) or any feed. Reports separate internal moves from external hires. Exact semantics (ct, 19 Oct 10:50Z): employees are every active person member, whatever their roles; agents never apply. The people who run a job mark it internal-only. Applying creates a candidate with the member's name and email, source "Internal" (an automatic CRM-008 internal entry) and consent basis "internal application" with the current notice version; a candidate already holding that email is offered for LDG-005 duplicate review, never merged; a member applies to a job once. An internal application reaches only the owner, admins and the job's REQ-003 hiring team (its own hiring manager included): an interviewer or granted manager outside the team never sees it, even when assigned. The applicant sees only that they applied and when. The applicant's manager is the member's "reports to" (set by admins); the admins' notice setting is off by default, and when on that manager gets an in-app item naming who applied to which job (email is COM-001's). REPORT-001's openings report counts internal applications and internal moves (hires from internal applications) apart from external applications and hires. Each feed (ATTR-002, ATTR-003, ATTR-006, ATTR-010, ATTR-011) keeps internal-only jobs out in its own text and acceptance. **Acceptance evidence:** Post one internal-only and one public job; as an employee member see both and apply to the internal one; confirm it is absent from /jobs, the ATTR-008 embed, the ATTR-009 board API's list, detail and department counts, and every feed, carries source "Internal", is invisible to another employee, and REPORT-001 counts it as an internal move.

- **ATTR-006 — Niche and campus boards: Handshake, Wellfound, Built In and Dice.** Post approved jobs to Handshake (campus and early careers), Wellfound (startups), Built In and Dice (technology) through each board's employer integration, feed or partner API, with the same external ID, status readback, close-on-close, source attribution and visible USD cost for paid slots as ATTR-002. Each board or campaign it brings applications from joins the CRM-008 source list automatically (with its type), as the application page's source does today, and credit follows CRM-008. Internal-only jobs (ATTR-005) never reach it. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect an authorized employer account on each board, publish an approved engineering role and an internship, read back each live listing, apply through each and see per-board attribution; close both jobs and confirm removal on every board. Each board's source appears on the CRM-008 source list without an admin adding it. An internal-only job (ATTR-005) never appears. Each ATTR-012 job post with one of these boards among its destinations is its own listing there.

- **ATTR-007 — English and Spanish job posts and applications.** Recruiters publish a job post and its application questions in English and Spanish, with further languages through the same mechanism. Exact semantic (ct, 19 Oct 13:38Z and 15:09Z): a job is in California when its location, its offices or the post's offices name California or CA (HIRE-007's place rule). Lodge drafts the CPRA notice from what it collects (identifiers; professional information; sensitive information: accommodation requests and the voluntary gender answer, used only for those purposes), with purposes and retention per category, but the sale, sharing and sensitive-use statement is the business's own: the owner adopts the notice once, confirming "We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes beyond those the CPRA permits", before a California job publishes ("Adopt your California privacy notice first"); each later edit is a new version. Lodge offers no sale or sharing of personal information, so no opt-out or limit link exists; a capability that adds one carries those links before it ships. Every door that takes a California applicant's details shows the adopted notice and records its version on the candidate, application and receipt beside the general notice version, refusing a stale one: the hosted page, the ATTR-008 embed, the ATTR-009 board API (`californiaNotice` on the job, `cpraNoticeVersion` on the submission), ATTR-005 internal applications and CRM-009 capture at an event in California; ATTR-002/003/006/010/011 feeds and CAND-001 inherit it before they ship. Until CAND-001 ships, the public page `/privacy-requests` takes access, deletion and correction requests (honeypot; 10 attempts per connection per hour, kept as HMAC digests). Identity is verified by an application receipt code plus the email on that record (labelled "verified by receipt code and email on record"); an unverified request is kept as "identity not verified", linked to no one, and releases or changes nothing. Each request gets a private status page, shown once, that acknowledges it on receipt (the CPRA allows 10 business days), says how it will be processed and which identity check was used, and shows its deadline: 45 calendar days after the day it was received in the business's time zone, extendable once by up to 45 more days with a reason the requester sees. Access is fulfilled through the LDG-012 export, correction by the admin's correction and a completion note, deletion through LDG-012 erasure (after which the status page keeps only a personal-data-free "Your records were deleted"); a deletion an active legal hold covers is refused with that documented reason, and one a record the law requires the business to keep blocks is refused with that reason and the admin's explanation (REPORT-002 names those records when it ships). Lodge sends no email about requests until COM-001 sends; COM-001 adds the acknowledgement email then. **Acceptance evidence:** Apply as a California applicant and see the CPRA notice version recorded; submit verified access and correction requests, see their deadlines and fulfill them; request deletion of a held record and receive the documented refusal reason.

## Push further

- **COORD-001 — Own stalled hiring coordination to completion.** A recruiting-coordinator agent joins authorized live inbox threads, calendar events, application stages and outstanding feedback to own stalled logistics with a named recruiter, next action/deadline and durable progress. Under recruiter-approved outreach cadence, recipient scopes and USD budgets it gathers availability, follows candidate replies, repairs reschedules, chases scorecards and proposes replacement panellists. It verifies the actual booking, reply or submitted feedback and escalates blockers until completion or an explicit stop; a generated task is not completion. Candidates choose slots, recruiters approve plan/panel changes and humans alone advance, reject or offer. Source changes, lost grants, opt-out, withdrawal and uncertain provider effects pause unsafe work and remain visible. **Acceptance evidence:** Leave a candidate reply, panel availability and scorecard stalled; enable a reviewed policy, receive real reminders, reply with changed availability and remove a panellist. Watch the agent reconcile a reschedule, obtain approval for replacement, verify feedback and close each task by readback across restart; deny a protected-data prompt and inspect escalation when the provider is unavailable.

- **COORD-002 — Diagnose avoidable hiring-process delays.** An agent joins authorized stage histories, mailbox response times, scheduling attempts, survey feedback and offer outcomes to distinguish candidate waits, recruiter queues, panel bottlenecks and provider failures. It proposes a specific workflow change with cited cases, uncertainty, sample limits and a measurable success criterion. The recruiting lead approves a sandbox trial and activation; comparisons retain cohort definitions and do not infer protected traits, rank suitability or rewrite past decisions. **Acceptance evidence:** Use several delayed hiring cases with different causes, request a diagnosis and inspect cited event/thread evidence and sample limits; approve one reminder-policy trial in the sandbox, measure the declared delay metric and reject another proposal without any production change.

- **COORD-003 — Close accepted-offer handoff exceptions.** An agent compares current approved terms, signed-document readback from whichever OFFER-002–OFFER-006 path was used, employer-reviewed Checkr or HIRE-006 provider status and the HIRE-001–HIRE-005 destination employee readback (Willow, BambooHR, ADP, Gusto, Rippling, Paylocity, Justworks, Paycom or UKG). It gathers missing permitted fields, proposes exact corrections, owns deadline/escalation and verifies that the same person, job, pay and start date reached HR once. A named human approves legal terms, compensation, adverse actions and new employee creation; ambiguous identity, unsigned changes and provider rejection remain exceptions. The agent never self-grants HR access or marks a file export as a completed hire. **Acceptance evidence:** Accept a signed offer with a missing HR field and then return a mismatched destination employee; watch the agent cite the discrepancy, obtain the required correction/creation approval, reconcile one destination and close only after exact readback, including after response loss or revocation.

- **COORD-004 — Evidence-led sourcing with controlled outreach.** An agent researches permitted licensed-source and prior-pool data against recruiter-selected job-related criteria, assembles prospects with source passages and contact permission, and drafts individualized outreach and screening bookings. The recruiter approves the audience, claims, exact templates, allowed cadence and budget, including from scoped Slack approval. Replies, opt-outs and stale source facts stop follow-up. No protected-trait inference, suitability ranking, automatic rejection or fabricated experience enters the recommendation; people choose whom to interview. **Acceptance evidence:** Give an approved role and a licensed prospect/pool dataset, inspect source-backed prospect facts and draft outreach, approve a small campaign in Slack and receive a reply; revoke a source and opt out a second prospect, verifying stopped contact and no automatic application decision.

- **COORD-005 — Keep every candidate informed.** An agent watches every active application for silence beyond the employer's promised response window (for example five business days after a stage change or interview). Using recruiter-approved status templates it drafts an individual update, or the DISP-001 rejection email a person already decided, and queues it for one-click recruiter approval; only templates the recruiting lead pre-approved for this purpose send without that click. It verifies COM-001 delivery, stops for opt-out, withdrawal or legal hold, never decides progression or rejection and never invents a reason. A weekly report shows candidates kept informed and remaining silences. **Acceptance evidence:** Leave three candidates silent past the window (one already rejected awaiting email, one waiting on a debrief, one opted out); drafts appear for the first two only; approve one and let the pre-approved status update send; verify delivery readback; the opted-out candidate receives nothing and no stage changes.

- **COORD-006 — Cited debrief briefs from released evidence.** After feedback release an agent prepares a debrief brief for the hiring manager: each criterion's ratings with the interviewers' own evidence quoted and linked, where interviewers agree or disagree, criteria nobody assessed and questions to resolve, with no combined score, recommendation or protected-trait reference. It reads only released scorecards the manager can see and cites exact passages; unsupported statements are refused. The manager edits and keeps or discards it; it never moves the candidate. Exact semantic: Lodge runs no model, so the brief is the preparing agent's (or person's) statements, each of kind evidence, agreement, disagreement, gap or question, checked against the latest submitted version of each released scorecard the preparer can see. A citation quotes at least 8 characters, case-sensitively and exactly, from that card's evidence for the criterion or its key take-aways; every statement except a gap cites at least one. Ratings 1–2 are below the bar and 3–4 at or above it: interviewers agree on a criterion when two or more rate it and all fall on one side, and disagree when ratings fall on both sides; an agreement or disagreement statement must quote interviewers on the side(s) it names, and a gap names only a criterion nobody rated. A brief that leaves any disagreement or unassessed criterion unnamed is refused. A statement or quote that uses a combined-score or recommendation term, or a protected-trait term (the fixed lists in `src/lodge/agents/debriefs.ts` and `src/lodge/core/protected.ts`), is refused. The brief is visible to the job's managers, admins who run it and their agents, never to interviewers; it is marked out of date once a quoted scorecard is corrected (SCORE-002); manager edits pass the same checks; discarding deletes it, and erasure (LDG-012) deletes it with the candidate's other records. **Acceptance evidence:** Release three scorecards with one disagreement and one unassessed criterion and request a brief: every statement cites a released passage, the disagreement and gap are named and no overall score appears; a brief requested before release is refused.
